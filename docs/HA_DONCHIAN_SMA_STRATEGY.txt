STRATEGY OVERVIEW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Indicators (calculated on Heikin Ashi candles):
  • Heikin Ashi candles (smoothed price action)
  • Donchian Channel 20-period (highest/lowest over 20 bars)
  • SMA 50-period (trend filter)

Core Logic:
  LONG Entry:  DC mid crosses ABOVE SMA50
  LONG Exit:   DC mid crosses BELOW SMA50

  SHORT Entry: DC mid crosses BELOW SMA50
  SHORT Exit:  DC mid crosses ABOVE SMA50

Dynamic Counter-Trade Feature:
  • When a trade exits at a LOSS via signal → trigger counter-trade
  • Counter-trade enters at NEXT bar in OPPOSITE direction
  • Counter-trade TP = abs(previous_loss_pct) × 1.5
  • Example: Lost -4% on LONG → Enter SHORT with +6% TP
