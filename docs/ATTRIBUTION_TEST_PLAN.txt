STRATEGY: RSI MEAN REVERSION WITH TREND FILTER
================================================================================

**Strategy Logic:**
This is a classic mean reversion strategy that buys oversold conditions in an
uptrend and sells overbought conditions or when trend reverses.

**Why This Strategy:**
- Multi-condition entry (tests AND logic and signal strength calculation)
- Uses multiple indicator types (trend + oscillator)
- Clear market context (only trades with trend)
- Should generate mixed win/loss trades (tests alpha calculation)
- Different signal strengths (tests strong vs weak signal hypothesis)

**Indicators:**
1. SMA 50 - Short-term trend filter
2. SMA 200 - Long-term trend filter
3. RSI 14 - Overbought/oversold oscillator

**Entry Conditions (AND logic):**
1. Price > SMA 200 (bullish long-term trend)
2. SMA 50 > SMA 200 (bullish short-term trend)
3. RSI < 30 (oversold - buy opportunity)

**Exit Conditions (OR logic):**
1. RSI > 70 (overbought - take profit)
2. SMA 50 crosses below SMA 200 (trend reversal - exit)

**Signal Strength Analysis:**
- Strong signals (3/3 conditions): All trend filters + deep oversold
- Medium signals (2/3): Trend OK but RSI borderline
- Weak signals (1/3): Only one condition met (shouldn't happen with AND)

================================================================================
KNOWN LIMITATIONS (PHASE 1A)
================================================================================

**What Phase 1A Does NOT Do (deferred to Phase 1B):**
- Indicator-aware signal strength (currently uses simple confluence)
- Bounded oscillator strength (RSI extremes not weighted)
- Crossover strength (how strong/recent the cross was)
- Momentum-based strength (acceleration not considered)
- Volatility adjustment (ATR-based weighting)

**Phase 1A Signal Strength:**
Current: conditions_met / total_conditions
Example: 3/3 conditions = 1.0 strength, 2/3 = 0.667 strength

**Phase 1B Signal Strength (future):**
Indicator-aware: RSI at 20 (very oversold) = higher strength than RSI at 29
Crossover-aware: Recent MACD cross = higher strength than old cross
